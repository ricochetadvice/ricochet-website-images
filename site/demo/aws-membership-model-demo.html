About this demo. The American Widget Society is fictional. Starting values are set near association benchmarks so the model behaves like a real membership book: 7,700 members, $1.84 million in dues, and a slow drift down with no changes. Fixed flows each year: 20% of renewing students move up to young professional, 12% of young professionals to Regular, and 1.5% of Regular members to Retired.
